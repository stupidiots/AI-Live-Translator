Privacy Policy — AI Live Translator

Effective date: November 27, 2026

AI Live Translator is developed by cryptogod. This policy explains what data the app does and does not handle.

No developer servers, no accounts

The developer operates no servers for this app and offers no user accounts. The developer does not collect, store, or sell your personal data, and the app contains no analytics or tracking SDKs from the developer.

Your API key

The app works with your own Google Gemini API key. Your key is stored only on your device and is sent only to Google when you use the translation features. The developer never receives your key.

Audio and translation

Audio is captured only while a translation session is running and only after you approve Android's on-screen consent prompt. Audio is sent to Google's Gemini API for translation and is processed under Google's API terms. Optional session transcripts are saved as text files on your device only.

Advertising

The app is free and supported by ads provided by a third-party advertising network (Adsterra). That network may use cookies or device identifiers to serve ads, under its own privacy policy. The developer does not control and does not receive that data.

Permissions

The app requests microphone and audio-capture permissions only to perform live translation, and notification permission only to show the running-session notification.
